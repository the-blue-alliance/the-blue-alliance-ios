Hey y'all - this release brings The Blue Alliance up to date with the new iOS 26 design: a glass tab bar, a dedicated Search tab, and Teams and Settings under a new More tab. Team lists have a filter again, Championship division events link to each other and to the event their winners advance to, match pages show scheduled, predicted, and actual start times, and the myTBA sign-in screen got a refresh. Under the hood the app has been rebuilt for Swift 6, and signing out of myTBA now reliably stops push notifications on that device.

The Blue Alliance is built and maintained by volunteers - help us build The Blue Alliance for iOS at https://github.com/the-blue-alliance
