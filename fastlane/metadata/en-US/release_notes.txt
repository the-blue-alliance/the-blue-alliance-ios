Hey y'all - this release brings The Blue Alliance up to date with the new iOS 26 design: a glass tab bar that tucks away as you scroll, a dedicated Search tab, and Teams and Settings under a new More tab. Team lists have a filter again, Championship divisions link to each other, match pages show scheduled, predicted, and actual start times, and match breakdowns show the fouls each alliance committed. myTBA has a refreshed sign-in screen, stays in sync when you come back to the app, and no longer crashes when a favorite is saved twice. Signing out of myTBA now reliably stops push notifications on that device. There's also a new Kickoff app icon in Settings → App Icon.

The Blue Alliance is built and maintained by volunteers - help us build The Blue Alliance for iOS at https://github.com/the-blue-alliance
