Hey y'all - this release brings The Blue Alliance up to date with the new iOS 26 design: a glass tab bar that tucks away as you scroll, a dedicated Search tab, and Teams and Settings under a new More tab. Team lists have a filter again, Championship divisions link to each other and to the event their winners advance to, and match pages show scheduled, predicted, and actual start times. myTBA has a refreshed sign-in screen, a star that shows when something is a favorite, and favorites that stay in sync when you come back to the app. Signing out of myTBA now reliably stops push notifications on that device.

The Blue Alliance is built and maintained by volunteers - help us build The Blue Alliance for iOS at https://github.com/the-blue-alliance
